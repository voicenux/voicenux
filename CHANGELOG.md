# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Welcome to Voicenux, the voice changer for Linux.

### Highlights

- 68 voices in seven categories, from Baby and Elderly to Demon, Robot, Underwater and Auto-Tune
- Background sounds like rain, bubbles, a crowd or a campfire
- The Voicenux Microphone for Discord, OBS, games and every other app, or as your default microphone
- Hear myself, mute, noise suppression and a noise gate
- A soundboard with 14 starter sounds, your own files and drag and drop
- Voicelab: build your own voices from 25 effects
- Global shortcuts for voices, sounds and switches, a tray icon and a command line
- English and German
